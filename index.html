Data provenance

2023 Building Footprints ↗ Road Corridors ↗ Development Model Footprints ↗ Development Activity Monitor ↗ CLUE Small Areas ↗ OpenStreetMap lane tags ↗
The study area uses the official dataset centroids within 1 km of the market reference point. Development footprints and heights are indicative, as described by the City of Melbourne. Lane counts are mapped OpenStreetMap tags: unknown values remain “Not recorded” and are never inferred.
